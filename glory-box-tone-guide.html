Tone Guide No. 09
Glory Box
Portishead · Dummy, 1994
Rig Kemper Player · FR10
Guitarist Adrian Utley
Genre Trip-hop · 1994
Character Wah · Fuzz · Telephonic
Fender Postmodern Telecaster
Cunife Humbucker · Neck Pickup · Volume 8–9
Utley used a Fender Jazzmaster on Dummy — low-output, slightly hollow, with a compressed spongy character that is the opposite of a PAF humbucker. The Cunife neck humbucker in your Postmodern Tele is the closest match in your collection: low output, mid-scooped, clean-up-friendly, sitting sonically between a single coil and a vintage humbucker. The result through wah and fuzz is focused and papery — the right side of thick — matching the narrow, almost telephonic quality of the original solo tone. Back the volume off to 8 to soften the attack further.
BACKUP — H150 neck pickup, vol 7. Warmer and denser, slightly more woolly through fuzz but workable. Less of that Jazzmaster-adjacent hollowness.

LVL III Slot Routing — Expression Pedal as Wah

Expression pedal connected to the Kemper Player's dedicated pedal input. Assign it to the Wah effect in Slot A via the Kemper's pedal settings. Slot order: A — Wah · B — Kemper Fuzz · C — Mouse · D — spare. Post-stack: DLY and REV. The wah must sit in slot A to ensure it runs before the fuzz — the correct impedance interaction and filter-before-clipping behaviour depends on this order.

On Rig Exchange search: "Fender Twin Clean", "BF Twin", "SF Twin Reverb". Look for profiles described as dark, clean, or vibrato channel. Avoid profiles labelled bright or tweed. Utley ran the whole Dummy album through a Roland Space Echo preamp — the Single Delay's Grit and Flutter parameters in the DLY slot approximate this colouration.

Expression Pedal Setup — Slot A

In the Kemper Player's pedal settings, set the dedicated input to Wah Pedal and enable Bypass @ Heel so the wah disengages when the pedal is fully back — useful for the clean chord sections. The Wah 61 type (Vox AC30-era voiced) gives the narrower, more nasal peak that suits this tone over the wider Cry Baby-type models. The wah runs first in the pre-stack chain — filter before fuzz is essential to the telephonic character.

Kemper Fuzz — Slot B

The Kemper Fuzz (LVL II) models the Dunlop Fuzz Face. Use the silicon character setting for more aggressive, gated response over germanium's smoother sustain — silicon is closer to the unstable sputtering on the recording. Enable the Impedance Switch (LVL II exclusive) for the most authentic fuzz impedance behaviour. Goal is sputtering, compressed, slightly unstable fuzz — not smooth sustained distortion.

Mouse Stage Role — Slot C

Utley confirmed Fuzz Face into RAT as the Glory Box chain. The Kemper Mouse (ProCo RAT model, LVL II) cascades into the Kemper Fuzz output — adding harmonic density and compression, not primary dirt. Keep distortion under 5. Note the RAT/Mouse filter is inverted: turning it up increases brightness, not darkness. Bass and mids are deliberately shaved at the amp — top-end focus only produces the narrow AM radio character of the solo.

04 Expression Pedal / Wah — Usage
Usage Modes — Glory Box
▶ Primary — Verse & Solo
Active / Swept
Slow, deliberate sweeps throughout. Wah parked toward toe in the upper-mid register (~1.5–3kHz) giving that nasal, focussed quality. Live versions of Glory Box are heavy wah — Utley uses it almost continuously. Sweep slowly. Not a funk chop pattern — long, moaning arcs.
◎ Secondary — Chord sections
Cocked / Parked
Pedal parked at ~60–70% toe (upper-mid hump, around 2kHz). Gives the telephonic, narrow-bandwidth filter character on the sustained chord tones. Set and forget for these sections — no movement.

Space Echo Preamp — Key Detail

Utley has stated the entire Dummy album was run through a Roland Space Echo preamp — adding harmonic saturation and high-frequency roll-off that creates that aged, slightly lo-fi character. The Kemper Single Delay's Grit parameter is the direct equivalent: it applies tape saturation and distortion to the repeats. Combine with Flutter Intensity for mechanical tape drift. There is no dedicated "Tape Echo" type in the Kemper — the Single Delay with these parameters correctly dialled is the right tool.

06 Playing Notes
Intro / Verse Chords
Wah parked ~60% toe. Slow, heavy pick attack. Let chords sustain and decay through the fuzz — don't dampen. The fuzz and wah together create the narrow harmonic envelope. Neck pickup only.
Solo Character
Utley described it as tongue-in-cheek — Hendrix-influenced noise rather than technique. Slow bends, wide vibrato, wah swept actively. More about texture and sustain than melodic precision. Less is more.
Pick Attack
Medium-heavy. The Cunife humbucker will clean up quickly — keep pick attack consistent and let the fuzz do the compression. Avoid featherlight picking which reduces the fuzz gating character.
Volume Knob
Keep at 8–9. Rolling back slightly tames the fuzz input and shifts the character from saturated to compressed-sputtering — closer to the original. Full 10 risks too much fuzz density.
Vibrato Style
Wide and slow. Not fast classical vibrato — broad, arm-vibrato-like pitch movement even when done with fingers. Pairs with the wah sweep to produce that wailing, slightly detuned character.
Register
Stay in the upper-mid register (frets 12–17, B and e strings). This is where the cocked wah filter and fuzz interact most dramatically. Lower register loses the telephonic character.
