Kemper Advantage Over Clapp 15 Version
The Kemper path solves the biggest limitation of the Clapp guide. The wah is now real — expression pedal controls a Kemper Cry Baby wah effect set to Speer's cocked sweet spot using Pedal Mode Off + Manual knob. The wah can also be swept live via the expression pedal for expressive passages. With six slots, all six signal chain elements (wah, phaser, drive, compressor, reverb, delay) can run simultaneously in the correct order. The Kemper's Fender Deluxe Reverb profile captures the amp character more accurately than the Clapp 15's Class A voicing. This is the more accurate of the two guides.
Source: Premier Guitar Rig Rundown confirmed: Crybaby Fasel Wah (always on, cocked) → Boss PH-3 (Rate noon, Depth 3 o'clock, Res noon, Stage 8) → Boss DS-1 (Dist zero, Tone zero, Level max) → MXR Dyna Comp Mini (Output max, Sensitivity min) → EHX Holy Grail spring reverb (noon–1 o'clock) → Strymon El Capistan tape delay. Speer plays a single '70s Strat with DiMarzio Cruiser pickups (middle accidentally out of phase), D'Addario Chrome flatwound strings, and a worn Dunlop Green Tortex pick.
Tone Character

The Sound

Smooth, mellow, deeply reverberant. The flatwounds remove pick attack. The cocked wah adds a nasal mid-peak — vocal, almost dying. The DS-1 at zero distortion is a tonal shaper only. Everything floats in tape echo and spring reverb. The compressor glues it all together and adds the subtle swell response.

What to Aim For

Thai funk, dub, psychedelic soul. Clean but never clinical. Notes should melt rather than attack. The cocked wah gives single notes a vocal, nasal quality that no amount of EQ perfectly replicates — with the expression pedal, you finally have it. Play very lightly and let the chain do the work.

Guitar · Postmodern Stratocaster
Postmodern Stratocaster
Position 4 · Neck + Middle · Primary
Speer's Strat middle pickup is accidentally wired out of phase — creating a hollow, phase-cancelled Peter Green tone on positions 2 and 4. The Postmodern Strat has standard wiring so no out-of-phase effect is available, but positions 4 (neck + middle) and 2 (bridge + middle) give the closest in-between quack character. Position 4 is the primary position. Tone controls rolled back further than feels natural — the wah compensates for the absence of out-of-phase by providing its own mid-frequency character. Unmarked dials — clock positions.

Flatwounds strongly recommended: D'Addario Chrome flatwounds (.010–.046) on the Postmodern Strat fundamentally change the attack, sustain, and high-frequency character in a way that no EQ can replicate. With roundwounds the attack is too aggressive and the high end too bright even with tone controls rolled back. If you commit to this tone regularly, string the Strat with Chromes.

Kemper FX Slots · Full Chain · Level 3
Six Slot Configuration
Stomp A–D · FX Loop → X · Delay · Reverb
With six available slots, the complete Speer signal chain fits with nothing compromised. The wah runs in Stomp A — always engaged, expression pedal controlling sweep. Order mirrors Speer's actual chain as closely as the Kemper's architecture allows.
STOMP A
Cry Baby Wah
Wah Effect · Expression Pedal Controlled
Kemper Cry Baby or Whammy Wah model. Pedal Mode: On (expression pedal sweeps). Manual knob set to Speer's cocked sweet spot (~40–50% of sweep = mid-forward nasal peak). Leave pedal in this position for the cocked sound. Sweep for expressive passages.
EXP PEDAL
STOMP B
Phaser
Boss PH-3 Substitute · Always On
Kemper Phaser or Vintage Phaser type. Rate very slow, depth subtle. This is the Boss PH-3 role — slow, almost imperceptible sweep. If no phaser sounds right, use the Kemper Tremolo at very low depth as an alternative slow movement effect.
ALWAYS ON
STOMP C
DS-1 Style Drive
Boss DS-1 Substitute · Tonal Shaper · Always On
Kemper Vintage Drive or Transparent Drive. Gain near zero. Tone near zero (dark/rolled). Output/Level above unity. This is not a drive effect — it's a tonal shaper and buffer, replicating the DS-1 at zero distortion.
ALWAYS ON
STOMP D
Dyna Comp Compressor
MXR Dyna Comp Substitute · Always On
Kemper Compressor or Vintage Compressor. Output/Level high. Sensitivity/Attack very low — Speer keeps Sensitivity at minimum, Output cranked. The compressor glues the signal and adds the subtle swell response. Always on — essential to the tone.
ALWAYS ON
DELAY
Tape Echo
Strymon El Capistan Substitute · Always On
Kemper Legacy Delay or Tape Echo type. Quarter note timing ~400–450ms. Feedback 2–3 repeats. Mix prominent — the delay is a primary musical element, not background air. Expression pedal can morph Feedback for drone/swell effects if configured.
ALWAYS ON
REVERB
Spring Reverb
EHX Holy Grail Substitute · Always On
Kemper Spring Reverb or Natural Spring type. Mix medium-high — more present than other guides. Short decay, short predelay. Spring character with slight drip. Always on — baked into the Khruangbin sound.
ALWAYS ON
Slot order note: The Kemper places Stomps A–D before the amp, and Delay/Reverb after. This puts the wah, phaser, drive, and compressor pre-amp — correct for Speer's actual chain. The Kemper's internal Delay and Reverb run post-amp in the same position as Speer's Holy Grail and El Capistan. The order is architecturally accurate.
Wah Setup · Expression Pedal · Critical Settings
Kemper Wah · Expression Pedal
Most Important Setting In This Guide
This is the element that makes the Kemper version definitively better than the Clapp 15 version. Speer's wah is always engaged at a fixed cocked position — never swept during a song, never turned off. The expression pedal allows Bryce to reproduce this exactly: park it at the sweet spot and leave it there for the cocked tone, then sweep for expressive passages when desired.

Finding the sweet spot: Play a single note on the neck pickup and slowly rock the pedal from heel to toe. Listen for the nasal, vocal, almost-honky peak where the note takes on a singing quality. That's Speer's position. It'll be somewhere between 35–55% of the pedal's travel depending on your Kemper's wah calibration. Mark it with a small piece of gaffer tape on the pedal housing so you can reliably return to it.

Stomp C · Drive Settings
Drive / Tonal Shaper
DS-1 at Zero Distortion · Always On
Speer runs a Boss DS-1 with Distortion fully CCW, Tone fully CCW, Level fully CW. At these settings the DS-1 is a tonal shaper and buffer — not a drive pedal. The rolled Tone darkens and tightens the signal. Use a Kemper drive type with near-zero gain and rolled tone. The Kemper's Green Screamer, Vintage Drive, or Transparent OD types work — the goal is zero grit with slight tonal darkening.

Delay Slot · Tape Echo Settings
Tape Echo
El Capistan Substitute · Always On · Central to Sound
The Strymon El Capistan is always on. Speer uses an expression pedal to control feedback in real time, letting notes drone and swell against their own repeats without a looper. The Kemper delay slot — use Legacy Delay or Tape Echo type. Mix is prominent, feedback 2–3 repeats, tape character with moderate wear. Quarter note timing.

Expression pedal on feedback: If you want to replicate Speer's El Capistan trick, assign the Kemper Delay feedback parameter to the Morph (expression pedal). At heel the feedback stays at 35%, at toe it can swell toward 80–90% for drone effects. Rock the pedal forward during sustained notes and the repeats multiply — release and it decays back. This is one of Speer's core live techniques.
Reverb Slot · Spring Reverb Settings
Spring Reverb
Holy Grail Substitute · Always On
Speer's EHX Holy Grail is set to spring reverb mode at noon–1 o'clock. The Kemper Spring Reverb type is the direct match. Mix higher than other guides — the reverb is consistently present in Khruangbin's sound, more prominent than any of the Black Keys guides. Runs after the delay so the reverb tails bloom into the delay repeats.
